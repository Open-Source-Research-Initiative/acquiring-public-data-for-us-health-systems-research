## Authorization and deliverables

Feasibility covers protocol and search calibration, related-work and contribution assessment, eligibility and source-reference preparation, and a proposed eight-system pilot under the limits below. The current instruction authorizes preparation and research only: **do not execute selected software or download underlying health data now**. Any later execution requires the applicable human authorization and safety/resource review. Proposed allowance: **80 total pilot staff hours**, including reference preparation, execution, checking, review and reporting. Proposed review: **23 November 2026, America/New_York**, subject to human scheduling. Record human person-hours, agent elapsed time and metered costs separately using the [effort log](research/README.md#effort-accounting). The staff ceiling refers to human person-hours, including oversight; agent time is reported separately. Record actual hours by role and activity; the lead decides whether to pause before the cap. No spending or service registration follows from this protocol.

Deliverables are a versioned pilot plan and case inventory; bounded search and screening records; source and access evidence; separate execution and sealed assessment packets; feasibility attempt and review records if subsequently authorized; an empirical-overlap assessment; and a human decision memo stating whether to stop, revise or seek approval for a main evaluation. Single-human exploratory search, screening, source preparation and protocol drafting may proceed with that limitation disclosed. A second qualified human must independently review pilot eligibility, reference evidence and outcome classifications before independent-review claims or main-study progression. If unavailable, report single-human exploratory findings and leave the progression decision pending. Agent review is not independent human review.

## Question and boundaries

Map publicly inspectable implemented software that acquires external, publicly published, non-patient-level U.S. health-systems or population-health data. Provider and facility records are included; patient-level records, including public individual survey microdata, are excluded. Retain unavailable or restricted formerly public sources. Portals without inspectable acquisition code, offline parsers, validators and other resources form a companion map, not the acquisition denominator. Distinguish system, workflow, source family, source release or snapshot, software revision, evidence and attempt. A repository and paper describing one system are not independent systems.

The empirical question is whether a selected native workflow demonstrates acquisition of its specified public source under fixed conditions, and whether independently selected source facts are preserved in output. Execution success, verified acquisition, bounded fidelity, comparable same-input reproduction and substantive task fitness are distinct. Neither a successful exit nor a catalog record establishes data access, correctness, completeness or fitness. Source facts establish only bounded concordance with their source, not the truth of the source.

## Before pilot collection

Freeze and version the search manifest, eligibility rules, case selection, packet contents, resource limits, reference and comparison rules, and reviewer plan. Record deviations as dated amendments, preserving prior versions and results; exploratory cases remain labeled as such. Identify source families, exact queries and channels, search dates, result limits and errors, and deduplication evidence. The map is the declared search frame, not an internet census. The pilot is purposive: seek eight distinct eligible systems across at least three source families, including older, poorly documented and current-snapshot cases. Keep pilot cases out of any later primary evaluation. Fewer than eight confirmed cases cannot pass the eight-case progression gates without a prospectively documented redesign; do not fill with ineligible cases.

Complete the empirical-overlap check during feasibility, before proposing main-study workload. Inspect the closest accessible reviews and empirical studies, including the previously inaccessible 2026 open-source health technology review where available. Record search queries, dates, actual inspected material, access failures and a claim-by-claim comparison of population, acquisition verification, independent source checking and findings. An inventory overlap alone does not defeat the study; a study answering the same question in the same population requires a specific defensible extension or justified replication. State the surviving contribution and uncertainty; stop or redesign if none remains. Earlier proposal research is retained in the historical source ledger and candidate records as exploratory planning, not an approved protocol.

## Selection hierarchy

For each selected system, freeze the observation date and inspect official project releases and documentation without inspecting attempt outcomes. Select (1) the latest stable tagged public release available by that date; otherwise (2) the latest non-prerelease published release; otherwise (3) the default-branch commit at the observation cutoff. Record exact tag and commit or immutable digest, source and timestamp. Do not select a later revision because a prior choice fails. Use documentation versioned with that revision; if absent, use linked official revision-matched documentation, then a dated official current document with mismatch explicitly recorded. If no trustworthy matching documentation exists, retain the case and classify the limitation; do not infer instructions.

Enumerate documented native acquisition workflows from the selected revision before running, grouping parameter variants of the same entrypoint/source path as one workflow. Select uniformly with a recorded seed if multiple qualifying workflows exist; if none can be enumerated, retain a workflow-not-enumerable disposition. For the selected workflow choose the documented default task, source and parameters; if no default exists choose a clearly documented runnable example. If multiple equivalent examples remain, sort stable identifiers and use a recorded random seed. Use a bounded documented example when it is the selected default/example; otherwise use its documented full scope, subject to the fixed resource policy. For an unspecified source release use the documented current-as-of behavior with a predeclared observation window; a documented fixed release remains fixed. Where parameter values remain unspecified, choose an explicitly documented permissible value using a deterministic recorded rule, with rationale before execution. Do not use reference-derived expected values to choose parameters, change task scope after a result, substitute a source or replace an inconvenient workflow. Record unresolved discretion and adjudicate before execution.

## Separated packets and independent reference

The [execution packet](protocol/execution-packet.md) contains the selected native task, version, allowed public documentation, inputs/parameters and resource and safety rules. It contains **no expected records, reference values, fixtures, checker results or assessment feedback**. The [sealed assessment packet](protocol/assessment-packet.md) retains independently sourced expected observations, selected records, identity and completeness evidence, comparison rules, checker tests and limitations. Prepare the reference independently of the evaluated implementation and do not reveal it to the executor until the primary output and attempt log are locked. The human lead or designee controls access and records timestamps, roles and disclosure; a Markdown heading alone is not access control. If separation cannot be maintained, label the case contaminated and exclude any independence claim. If reference-derived guidance is intentionally supplied, record it as assistance, lock a separate phase and describe the changed experiment; never silently treat it as the original procedure.

Publisher manifests/checksums and independently captured observations may support release identity and bounded content; hashes show byte identity to retained material, not authenticity, completeness or truth. For a fixed release, preserve publisher identification, actual revision, retrieval date, hash and applicable dictionary. For a current-snapshot task, record URL, query and timestamps and independently compare matching observations near the acquisition event; lack of immutable release ID is not a failure if the bounded snapshot can be verified. Changing or unobservable inputs are unresolved or non-comparable. Do not infer a failed download from a legitimate empty result without independent evidence that data should be present; preserve missing, suppressed and zero distinctly. A publisher validator or evaluated program cannot be the sole expected-result oracle.

## Proposed attempt and outcomes

If later authorized, use a disposable unprivileged isolated environment and frozen documented instructions. Freeze environment, executor model and tools, documentation, task, and access conditions; record material human and agent actions. Permit documented configuration and at most one justified transient retry or one later diagnostic repeat, not both; preserve first outcome. No source-code patches, undisclosed troubleshooting, alternate providers, protected records, production access or maintainer contact in the primary phase. A diagnostic repeat is separately labeled and cannot replace the initial classification. Do not execute unsafe software. Apply per-case ceilings of three active executor hours, six elapsed hours, four VM-hours on up to four vCPUs, 16 GiB RAM, 100 GiB disk and 20 GiB network transfer, with separate proposed $10 compute/network and $10 model caps; these ceilings do not authorize spending. Reference acquisition counts toward case resource meters and total pilot staff hours.

The primary endpoint is **observed verified acquisition under the fixed procedure**, within the frozen full-release or bounded-query scope. Report V/N for selected eligible systems, terminal reasons and evidence. A finalized case with no independent comparator has no verified demonstration, while its correctness remains unknown; it is not automatically an incorrect output or software defect. U denotes unfinished demonstration classification at data lock; [V/N, (V+U)/N] bounds completion of that classification only, not underlying capability or correctness. A wrong release established by independent evidence is a task mismatch; source unavailability, access, safety or resource blocks are distinct. Full acquisition requires independently supportable completeness evidence; otherwise call the finding bounded. Report fidelity, same-input author-output reproduction, task constraints and their applicable/assessable/unknown denominators separately. Without a comparable author/reference output, reproduction is not assessable. Separate byte equality from any prespecified canonical equality and do not compare different source releases as same-input reproductions.

## Frame errors and future main sample

Any future main sample requires separate human approval after feasibility and protocol review. If approved, freeze the deduplicated eligible frame, exclude pilot and team-authored systems as documented, and use the prospectively fixed random selection rules. Retain every original selection, seed, timestamp and evidence. If independent review finds a selected item was genuinely ineligible at the frozen cutoff—for example documentation only, a duplicate, or outside the defined population—record the error and rationale, correct the frame and denominator, and adjudicate independently. Before draw, predefine reproducible ordered reserve selection from the corrected frame, using the original seed or a separately fixed seed; use it only to restore the intended sample size after a confirmed true frame error, and report original and corrected samples. A duplicate maps to its canonical system and is counted once. If the corrected frame contains no reserve, report the smaller sample. An eligible system that fails, is inaccessible, lacks instructions, is unsafe, is resource-limited or lacks an oracle is **not** a frame error and is never replaced. If the correction changes frame eligibility broadly, pause and rerun screening and sampling under a versioned amendment rather than quietly patching individual cases.

## Pilot decision rules

Proceed to a human main-study review only if all conditions are met:

- Eight eligible distinct-system cases were selected; at least six dispositions were independently adjudicated and at least six have an inspected, retained defensible reference or independently evidenced terminal barrier. A planned reference alone is insufficient.
- At least four cases across at least two source families have substantive independent source-to-output reference checks prepared, including acquisition-only returned-record checks where appropriate. At least **three actual assessable workflow-output comparisons across at least two source families** were completed and independently reviewed. Pass and fail both count; an unavailable output or prepared fixture alone does not. If unmet, keep fidelity exploratory or redesign; do not claim this gate passed.
- Total pilot staff time is at most **80 hours** and median total staff effort per case is at most **eight hours**, with preparation, execution, checking, independent review and reporting included. If the total cap is reached, stop and report incomplete work; do not hide hours or infer a pass.
- Independent qualified human review, safe isolation and feasible storage are available; search screening forecast and resource limits are acceptable. Complete the overlap check above. If more than four pilot cases are blocked primarily by study-imposed safety, runtime or resource limits, revise profile or scope before the main study.
- After the declared main search and screening, before sampling, at least ten eligible nonpilot, non-team systems remain for the proposed multi-system design; otherwise propose a smaller case study or map-only work. This is a diversity threshold, not statistical power.

Low workflow success is a possible finding, not grounds to replace cases. All thresholds are feasibility decisions, not evidence of scientific validity. Cole must review the completed evidence and explicitly authorize any later main evaluation, spending, scientific release or manuscript submission; reviewer appointment and actual review cannot be presumed.
