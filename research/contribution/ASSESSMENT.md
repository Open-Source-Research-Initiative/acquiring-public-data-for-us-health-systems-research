# Contribution assessment for the feasibility decision

17 November 2026. Coordinator adjudication of two parallel literature assessments, their follow-ups and the eight-case preparation. **Recommendation: continue the pilot as an independent empirical evaluation using established methods. Do not claim a new verification method, an exhaustive inventory, or priority as the first study.** Human acceptance remains pending.

## What survives the comparison

The useful question is: **Under fixed, documented conditions, what can an independent evaluator substantiate about the source identity and selected source facts in outputs of third-party public U.S. health-data acquisition workflows?**

The contribution would be the resulting cross-workflow evidence: which intended releases or snapshots can be verified, which selected facts agree or disagree with independent source observations, and which cases remain unassessable and why. Software execution, verified acquisition, bounded fidelity and research fitness remain different outcomes. An eight-case purposive pilot tests this measurement procedure; it cannot estimate the prevalence of reliable healthcare software.

This is worth pursuing even without a claim of methodological novelty. It makes an operational research question answerable with case-level evidence, including negative and unresolved findings. USHSO may benefit from those findings, but its architecture is neither the study population nor the success criterion.

## Completion and remaining access limits

The feasibility contribution assessment is complete enough to recommend a bounded pilot. It is an exploratory comparison, not a completed systematic review. One broad PubMed query returned 237 hits but retrieved only 20 identifiers; that truncated query is not a screening denominator. API identity verification does not establish the truth of an article's findings.

The full 2026 review, the harmonization article's version history and its actual supplement remain unresolved access items. No purchase, access-control bypass or external contact was attempted. Revisit them before a manuscript-level novelty statement; the present recommendation does not depend on their absence. If later evidence reveals an equivalent evaluation, describe a justified replication with explicit differences in source period, population and execution conditions, or narrow the study. Do not retain a novelty claim by silently changing definitions.

## What the pilot must demonstrate

Keep the existing progression requirement of at least three actual assessable source-to-output comparisons across at least two source families, alongside the remaining eight-case and review gates. Prepared fixtures and successful exits alone do not meet it. A discrepancy counts as an assessable comparison; missing output does not. If independent source identity or useful reference construction proves infeasible, publish a clearly bounded feasibility result or redesign the measurement rather than expanding outside healthcare merely to increase N.

The selected eight systems span seven primary source programs, with two workflows using NPPES. That dependence is recorded rather than counted as another independent source family. Their selection is purposive and informed by earlier documentation inspection, not random sampling from a frozen population. No runtime outcomes informed the current choices.
