## Resolved without requiring another user choice

- Metadata-only helpers and standalone generic demographics are outside the primary health-observation acquisition draw. Geography/measure variants sharing one source mechanism are grouped. Corrected draws and former proposals are preserved.
- Eight systems cover seven primary source programs; NPPES dependence is explicit. Zenodo hosting is not another source family. PricePortal's selected workflow is NPPES lookup, not price-file acquisition.
- No known-positive provider number, convenient date slice or easier workflow replaces a native task. An empty/error result is not automatically failed acquisition, verified emptiness or incorrect software.
- The existing three-comparison/two-family progression threshold remains. At most six source-derived check units per case are proposed in the assessment design; that is bounded checking, not an accuracy estimate.
- A synthetic access probe established that the current agent/tool boundary is inadequate. No candidate software ran. Source references and answers have therefore not been exposed to a purported blinded executor.
- The coordinator found a static TLS peer-verification concern in the pinned NPPES client and error-to-empty handling in PricePortal. These require preflight and careful outcome interpretation; neither was patched or demonstrated at runtime.

## Effort and readiness accounting

The 80-hour allowance is a ceiling, not a forecast or a finding that the study is cheap. A planning allocation is 20 hours for reference preparation, 16 for execution oversight/setup, 16 for checking, 12 for human review and 16 for shared coordination/reporting. Track actual human effort separately; allocate shared human effort equally across the eight selected cases when computing median per-case effort. Reaching the cap does not guarantee the median threshold passes.

Case-specific preparation has begun in this revision. Its human effort is unmeasured, not zero; the human lead must supply any actual review/oversight time before a complete cost claim. Earlier general proposal research remains unmeasured pre-pilot effort. Automated work intervals are recorded as observed agent activity, not converted into human hours. No pilot cost or effort gate is claimed passed.
