# Final PI review workflow

**Effective:** 17 November 2026, prospectively, under Cole Lyons's direction. This governs the review sequence for an assigned phase. It does not alter historical records, evidence thresholds, technical controls, selection scope, or the separate decision to authorize a main evaluation.

The coordinator completes all assigned model runs and checks before sending Cole one consolidated review packet. Record each assigned item as completed, blocked, failed, unavailable or unresolved, with reasons and evidence; preserve substantive disagreements and their implications. A blocked item is not a completed check. Routine intermediate human gates are superseded; decisions that exceed the authorized task remain with the responsible human. Model agreement is not independent human review or scientific approval.

The packet identifies:

- The phase, authorized scope, repository commit and date, assigned models and their roles, and the materials reviewed.
- Each assigned run and check, its outcome and evidence, including typed blocks, failures, limitations, unresolved questions and disagreements.
- The proposed findings and decisions, supporting and contrary evidence, deviations, and the recommendation to accept, revise or stop.
- Any actual human review and its reviewer, scope and date; otherwise explicitly state that independent human review has not occurred. Identify requirements still unmet and any decision reserved for later work.

Cole is the sole required human reviewer for the current phase, including the decision on main-study progression. He may request a second reviewer; a claim of independent human review requires actual qualified independent human review. PI sign-off and model cross-checks do not constitute independent human review. The substantive numerical and reference-evidence thresholds remain in force; main execution requires Cole's explicit final decision and authorization. A packet or model consensus does not grant it.
